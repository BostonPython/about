The [Urban Park Roof Garden](https://www.kendallcenter.com/events-public-programs/roof-garden/) is a roof-top public park in Kendall Square above the Red Line T stop.

Here is a 3D view of the garden looking to the south, four floors up nestled between the buildings of Kendall Square:

![3D view of the roof garden](./assets/images/up_roof_garden/up_view.png "A 3D view of the roof garden")

Here is a top-down view (north is at the top), showing where the park is between Main St and Broadway:

![Top-down view of the park](./assets/images/up_roof_garden/up_plan.png "Top-down view of the park")

There are two ways to get to the park, neither of them obvious. From Broadway, you can enter the parking garage to the right of the Marriot, and take the elevator to the top:

![The elevator entrance](./assets/images/up_roof_garden/up_elevator.png "The elevator entrance")

From Main street, there's a staircase up from the sidewalk, on the north side of the street just to the left of the T stop entrance in front of the Marriott:

![The stairs up to the park](./assets/images/up_roof_garden/up_stairs.png "The stairs up to the park")

Both approaches are marked with stylized UP logos, but those might be hard to use as landmarks.
