---
title: Full name registration
---

Boston Python holds events in many different locations, often company offices.
Policies vary from building to building. Some locations ask for names in
advance, and require full names that match your ID. Hosts differ in how
strictly they enforce this requirement. Some will refuse entry to people who
aren't on the list, or whose name doesn't match.

When needed, we will ask you for your full ID-matching name when you RSVP for
an event. If needed, we will close RSVPs two days before the event so that
we can provide an accurate list to the host.

There are a few ways to provide your full name:

- Check your [meetup.com profile](https://www.meetup.com/account/)
  to make sure your name agrees with your ID.
- If an event requires your full name, you will be asked for it when RSVPing.
- If you like, you can [contact a Boston Python organizer](contact) to provide
  the information for an event's RSVP list.  We'll make sure you're listed
  correctly.

We understand some members prefer to keep their personal information
private. We need to strike a balance between hosts' security requirements
and your privacy requirements. Get in touch with us at
leaders@bostonpython.com if we can help.
